The Book

A book you work through, not just read

Most self-help books pad a few ideas with long stories. Wisdom Through Psychology gives you one principle a day for 100 days, with active reading and journaling prompts that help you understand it, remember it and use it.

I

Self-Discovery

Spot the patterns you inherited and the ones you built to get by, so you can decide which ones still help you.

II

Mastery

Make small daily habits that add up. As the book says on day one, habits are easier than goals.

III

Empowerment

Use will as your compass. Willpower here isn't forcing yourself through discipline. It's putting your energy toward what matters to you.

About the Author

Arjun Kumar

Arjun Kumar is an author, educator and psychology scholar based in Highland, California. He holds degrees from UC Irvine and USC, and his work covers mindfulness, ethics and emotional well-being, the place where psychology meets everyday life.

His debut book, Wisdom Through Psychology: 100 Days of Transformative Wisdom, started as a decade of notes he kept on his phone. They were insights from his studies, from films and from daily life. He later shaped them into a 100-day series of prompts and practices that anyone can use without a background in psychology.

Kumar believes self-understanding isn't about fixing yourself. It's about understanding your patterns and choosing which ones still serve you. He is also the author of AMMOI – My Grandmother.
